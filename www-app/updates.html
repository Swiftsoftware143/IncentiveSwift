What’s new

Every change below is live in your console. Newest first.

20 November 2026

  • An opt-out that actually stops the mail. One click in any message records the address on a do-not-mail list, and every send honours it — including the programme’s own reminders.
  • The do-not-mail list is yours to see. It sits in the admin console under Do-Not-Mail; a failure to read it now shows as an error instead of an empty list.
  • Survey answers are exportable. The survey builder shows the respondent’s name and exports a survey’s responses to CSV.
  • Feedback you send gets worked. The team now answers from the feedback queue instead of only reading it.
  • The inbox opens without a second sign-in. The mail-client door carries your session through.
  • Honest sentences. A suppressed receipt is no longer reported as sent.

19 November 2026

  • Campaign pages name the business. A shared campaign link tells the visitor whose programme it is, even before the campaign can play.
  • Signup names the plans you actually sell. The plan picker reads the console’s own plan list instead of a fixed three.
