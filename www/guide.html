A comprehensive guide to IncentiveSwift — the complete platform for running engaging campaigns, qualifying leads with intelligent surveys, and integrating with your marketing stack. From viral giveaways to qualified lead generation, everything in one place.

🚀 Getting Started

IncentiveSwift is built around two core concepts: Campaigns (interactive giveaways and contests) and IQS (Intelligent Qualifying Surveys). You can use them independently or together — attach a survey as a gate before a campaign entry.

Quick Start

  1. Log In

    Go to https://incentiveswift.com and log in with your credentials. If you don't have an account, sign up first.

  2. Create a Campaign

    From the sidebar, click Campaigns → + New Campaign. Choose a type (start with Spin the Wheel), give it a name, and set your entry rules.

  3. Add Prizes

    Define what users can win — cash amounts, discounts, free shipping, or custom rewards. Configure the odds for each prize.

  4. Launch

    Toggle the campaign to Active. Your campaign is now live with a shareable link and embed code.

  5. Track Results

    Monitor entries, wins, and conversions in the Campaign detail view and Analytics section.

🔄 Spin the Wheel

The most popular campaign type. Users click a button, the wheel spins, and lands on a prize segment. Great for instant gratification giveaways, lead magnets, and promotional events. Configure segments with prize labels, colors, odds, and win/lose outcomes.

🎟️ Raffle

Users enter a raffle by submitting their information. Winners are drawn at a scheduled time. Best for longer-running campaigns where you want to collect entries over time and announce winners later. Set a draw date and time, and the system will automatically select winners.

🪙 Scratch Card

A virtual scratch-off card. Users "scratch" (click/hover) to reveal hidden prizes. Three tiers allow for engaging reveal mechanics. Perfect for mobile audiences and email campaigns where quick interaction is key.

🎰 Slots

3-reel slot machine with spinning animation. Configure symbols on each reel and set match rules for wins. Three matching symbols = grand prize, two matching = small prize. High engagement, especially for gamified experiences.

📝 Quiz

Multiple-choice quiz where users answer questions to qualify. Configure correct answers, scoring, and outcomes. Great for educational content, product knowledge tests, and qualifying leads by knowledge level.

🎡 Wheel of Fortune

Similar to Spin the Wheel but with multi-tier mechanics — users can spin multiple times, unlock bonus rounds, or chain spins. More complex prize structures for advanced campaigns.

Configuring Credit Rate

The credit rate determines how many credits a customer earns per $1 spent. The minimum value is 1 (1 credit per dollar) and the maximum is 1000 (1000 credits per dollar). The default rate is 10. Set the credit rate per tenant in Settings → Loyalty.

Credit Formula

credits_earned = floor(purchase_amount * credit_rate)

For example, with a credit rate of 10, a $25.50 purchase earns floor(25.50 * 10) = 255 credits. The minimum credit award is always 1 credit, so any purchase of $0.10 or more earns at least 1 credit.

Tip: The credit rate is applied at verification time. Changing the rate affects future verifications only — historical transactions are not recalculated.

Purchase PIN

Every tenant is assigned a Purchase PIN automatically on signup. The PIN is a sequential identifier in the format Z100, Z101, Z102, and so on. This PIN is required for purchase verification — merchants enter it when verifying a customer's purchase.

The Purchase PIN is found in Settings → Loyalty. It is read-only in the UI. If a PIN is compromised, contact an admin to request a reset. PIN resets are a manual database operation with a full audit trail.

Security: Never share your Purchase PIN publicly. Treat it like a shared secret between your tenant and your point-of-sale or verification process. If you suspect it has been exposed, request a reset immediately.

Creating Offers

Offers are discount incentives that customers can redeem using their earned credits. Create and manage offers from Loyalty → Offers.

Offer Fields

FieldDescriptionConstraints
NameDisplay name for the offerRequired, max 255 characters
Discount PercentPercentage discount off the purchase1–100
Cap DollarsMaximum dollar value of the discountMust be greater than 0
ActiveToggle offer active/inactiveOnly active offers appear during verification

How Caps Work

The discount amount is calculated as min(purchase_amount × discount_percent / 100, cap_dollars). For example, a 20% offer with a $10 cap on a $100 purchase gives a $10 discount (capped). The same offer on a $30 purchase gives a $6 discount (below cap).

Credits are redeemed based on the cap: credits_redeemed = cap_dollars × 100. This means 1 credit = $0.01 of discount value. The redeemed credits are deducted from the customer's balance.

Purchase Verification Flow

The purchase verification process works step by step at the point of sale:

  1. Customer shows QR code

    The customer presents their unique loyalty QR code from their account portal. This identifies them to the merchant.

  2. Merchant scans QR

    The merchant scans the QR code using the IncentiveSwift verification interface (mobile or web). The system identifies the customer account.

  3. Enter purchase amount + PIN

    The merchant enters the purchase total and their tenant's Purchase PIN. The PIN authenticates the merchant and links the transaction to the correct tenant.

  4. Optional offer selection

    The merchant may select an active offer to apply. If no offer is selected, the customer simply earns credits on the purchase.

  5. Verify

    Click Verify to process the transaction. The system calculates: credits_earned = floor(amount × credit_rate). If an offer was selected, the discount is applied, credits redeemed are deducted from the customer's balance, and the offer name is logged in the transaction record.

Transaction record: Every verification creates a permanent transaction record showing the amount, credits earned, any offer applied, credits redeemed, and the merchant who processed it.

Voucher System

When a customer's purchase verification earns credits above a configurable threshold (e.g., 100 credits earned in a single transaction), the system may auto-issue a voucher. Vouchers represent special rewards or bonus offers.

Vouchers are listed in the customer's portal under Loyalty → My Vouchers. Each voucher shows its value, expiration date, and status (active/claimed/expired). To use a voucher, the customer clicks Claim to receive a unique claim code they can present at checkout.

Note: Voucher thresholds are configurable per tenant. Contact your admin to adjust the threshold amount or disable auto-issuance.

Referral System

The referral system encourages customers to bring in new business. Every participant gets a referral code (e.g., REF-JOHN-ABC123) they can share with friends.

When a referred friend signs up and completes their first purchase verification, the referrer earns bonus credits. The bonus amount is configurable per tenant. Referral history is visible in the customer portal under Loyalty → Referrals.

How It Works

  1. Get your code

    Share your referral code from the Loyalty → Referrals section of your account.

  2. Friend signs up

    The referred friend enters (or is tagged with) your referral code during account creation.

  3. First purchase

    When the friend completes their first purchase verification, the system credits your account with the bonus amount.

  4. Track earnings

    View your referral history, bonus credits earned, and pending referrals in the Referrals dashboard.

➕ Creating Campaigns

Basic Setup

  1. Name & Type

    Give your campaign a name and select the campaign type. You can also add a description and custom slug for the public URL.

  2. Prizes

    Define prize tiers. Each prize has a label, type (cash, discount, free_shipping, custom), value, and probability weight. Higher weight = more likely to win.

  3. Entry Rules

    Set how many times a user can enter (once per day, once total, unlimited). Optionally require email verification or phone number.

  4. Schedule

    Set a start and end date. Campaigns are only active within this window. You can manually toggle active/inactive anytime.

  5. Theme

    Customize the colors and appearance to match your brand. The campaign player will automatically use your theme colors.

  6. Save & Launch

    Click Create Campaign, then toggle Active to publish. Your campaign now has a public play URL.

Attaching an IQS Survey (Optional)

You can gate any campaign with an IQS survey. When enabled, users must complete the survey before they can enter the campaign. To attach a survey:

  1. Open Campaign Editor

    Edit an existing campaign or create a new one.

  2. Select Survey

    Find the Qualifying Survey section in the editor. Choose an IQS funnel from the dropdown, or leave it as "None" to skip.

  3. Save

    Click Save. Now when users visit the campaign link, they'll complete the survey first. Only qualified respondents (based on score thresholds) get access to the campaign.

How it works: When a campaign has an attached IQS funnel, the play page detects it automatically. Users see the survey questions first, then based on their score (qualified/disqualified), they're either allowed to enter the campaign or shown a disqualified message with optional redirect.

📋 IQS — Intelligent Qualifying Surveys

IQS (Intelligent Qualifying Survey) is IncentiveSwift's advanced survey engine. Use it to qualify and score leads, collect structured data, and push results into your CRM — all before they enter a campaign or independently as a stand-alone survey.

❓

12+ Question Types

Single choice, multiple choice, text, email, phone, rating, date, file upload, consent, slider, dropdown, image choice, matrix/grid

📊

Scoring & Tags

Each answer option has a score value. Total score determines outcome. Tags are auto-applied to leads in CoreSwift CRM

🔥

Auto-Classification

Respondents are automatically classified as Hot, Warm, or Cold based on their score percentage

🔀

Conditional Branching

Show or hide questions dynamically based on previous answers. Create smart, adaptive surveys

Creating a Survey Funnel

  1. Navigate to IQS

    Click IQS in the main sidebar (not the admin menu). Click + New Funnel to create a survey.

  2. Funnel Settings

    Give your funnel a name and description. Optionally enable Auto-Classification to automatically sort respondents into Hot/Warm/Cold based on score thresholds.

  3. Add Questions

    Click + Add Question. Choose a question type, write the question text, and configure options (for choice types). Each option has a Score and Tag that applies when selected.

  4. Set Outcomes

    Define outcome ranges for Qualified and Disqualified based on total score percentage. Configure redirect URLs and thank-you messages for each outcome.

  5. Save & Publish

    Save the funnel and toggle it Active. Your survey is now available for standalone play, widget display, or campaign attachment.

Survey Play Modes

IQS surveys support two play modes that users can choose from:

  • Step by Step — One question at a time with smooth transitions. Best for longer surveys where you want to maintain focus.
  • All at Once — All questions displayed on a single page. Best for short surveys where speed is important.

Question Options: Scores & Tags

Every choice-type question option has two extra fields:

  • Score — A numeric value assigned when this option is selected. The total score across all questions determines the respondent's qualification outcome.
  • Tag — A text label that gets applied to the respondent's profile in CoreSwift CRM. Tags make it easy to segment and filter leads later.

Example: On a question "What's your budget?" — Option "Under $500" has score 5, tag "budget_low". Option "$500-$2000" has score 15, tag "budget_mid". Option "Over $2000" has score 25, tag "budget_high".

🔥 Auto-Classification (Hot/Warm/Cold)

Auto-Classification automatically sorts respondents into lead tiers based on their survey score percentage. Enable it in your funnel settings.

How It Works

When enabled, the system calculates the respondent's final score as a percentage of the maximum possible score. It then matches the percentage against your configured ranges:

ClassificationScore RangeBadgeAction
🔥 Hot80% – 100%HotHigh priority — push to CRM immediately, auto-assign to hot pipeline stage
🟡 Warm50% – 79%WarmNurturing — enters standard pipeline, scheduled follow-up
🔵 Cold0% – 49%ColdLong-term — saved for future campaigns or drip nurture

Configuration

In the funnel editor, enable Auto-Classification. Three default ranges (Hot/Warm/Cold) are pre-configured but fully customizable. For each range you can set:

  • Label — The name of the classification (e.g. "Hot", "VIP", "Tier 1")
  • Minimum Score % — The lower bound of this range (0-100)
  • Tag — A tag automatically applied to the respondent's profile (e.g. "hot_lead", "vip_prospect")
CRM integration: Classified leads are automatically pushed to CoreSwift CRM with their classification tag and score. Hot leads appear in your pipeline's high-priority stage.

🔀 Conditional Branching

Conditional Branching lets you create smart surveys that adapt to each respondent's answers. Questions appear or hide based on what was answered previously.

How It Works

Each question can have a condition (show_if) that controls whether it's displayed:

OperatorDescriptionExample
equalsShow if previous answer exactly matches valueShow "What's your budget?" only if "Are you interested?" = "Yes"
not_equalsShow if previous answer does NOT matchShow "Why not?" only if "Interested?" ≠ "Yes"
containsShow if previous answer contains the value (for multi-select)Show follow-up if answer contains "Premium"

Setting Up Conditions

  1. Open Question Editor

    When creating or editing a question, scroll to the Advanced section.

  2. Enable Condition

    Toggle "Show this question only if..." — this reveals the condition fields.

  3. Select Dependency

    Choose which previous question this depends on from the dropdown. All questions with a lower sort order are available.

  4. Set Operator & Value

    Choose equals/not_equals/contains and enter the value to match against.

  5. Save

    The condition is stored. When the survey is played, this question only appears if the condition is met.

Example: A car dealership survey: "Do you currently own a vehicle?" → No → Hide "What make and model?" and "How many miles?". Instead show "What type of vehicle are you looking for?". The survey stays relevant and short for every respondent.

🎯 Survey + Campaigns

You can attach an IQS survey as a qualification gate before any campaign type. This creates a two-step flow: survey first, then campaign for qualified respondents.

How the Flow Works

  1. User visits campaign link

    The system detects an attached IQS funnel and redirects to the survey play page instead of the campaign directly.

  2. User completes survey

    All question types, scoring, classification, and branching apply normally. The system calculates total score and compares against outcome thresholds.

  3. If Qualified

    The user is redirected to the campaign play page where they can enter normally (spin the wheel, scratch the card, etc.). Their survey score and tags flow with them.

  4. If Disqualified

    The user sees a thank-you/disqualified message with an optional redirect URL (e.g., "Thanks for your interest — here's a consolation offer").

Use Cases

  • VIP Giveaways — Only qualified leads can enter the prize drawing
  • Product Launches — Survey to gauge interest, then offer a discount spin to high-scorers
  • Lead Gen Campaigns — Collect qualification data before allowing entry, pushing scores + tags to CoreSwift
  • Event Registration — Qualify attendees before they enter a raffle for a free ticket
Tip: Set your Qualified threshold carefully. Too high and you'll disqualify too many. Too low and the survey gate loses its purpose. Start around 60% and adjust based on analytics.

✅ Post-Submit Flow

After a survey is completed, you control exactly what happens next. Configure different outcomes and responses per qualification level.

Outcome Configuration

In the funnel editor, under Outcomes, set:

  • Qualified Range — The minimum score % threshold (default 60%)
  • Qualified Message — A themed thank-you message shown to qualified respondents (e.g. "Great news! You qualify for our special offer.")
  • Disqualified Message — Message for those who don't meet the threshold (e.g. "Thanks for your interest — we'll be in touch.")
  • Redirect URLs — Optional URLs for each outcome. After 3 seconds, the user is automatically redirected. Leave blank for no redirect.
No redirect: If no redirect URL is set, users see a clean themed thank-you page that matches their survey experience. No page jump, smooth transition, branded to your campaign's colors.

📈 Marketing Boost

Marketing Boost is an integrated email/SMS marketing dispatch service. When a user wins a prize or enters a campaign, Marketing Boost can automatically send them a targeted email or SMS offer.

Setting Up Marketing Boost

  1. Go to Provider Keys

    Navigate to Integrations → Provider Keys and add your Marketing Boost API key.

  2. Create Integration

    In the Integration Hub, add a new target of type Marketing Boost. Enter your Offer ID in the metadata section.

  3. Link to Campaign

    Open a campaign, scroll to Integrations, and toggle the Marketing Boost integration on. Optionally set a per-campaign Offer ID override.

  4. Done

    Every time a user wins or enters that campaign, the integration fires automatically with the configured Offer ID.

Key Resolution Chain

When dispatching to Marketing Boost, the system resolves the API key in this order:

  1. Integration Target's own key — if set directly on the target
  2. Portfolio company settings — if the target is shared by a portfolio company
  3. Provider Keys table — the user's global Marketing Boost key

📡 Webhooks & Security

Webhook integrations let you send data to any external service when events happen in IncentiveSwift.

Webhook Security

All webhook targets are protected by a security layer:

  • Domain Allowlist — Only webhooks to approved domains are sent. Configure allowed domains per integration target.
  • Daily Limit — Set a maximum number of webhook calls per day to prevent abuse.
  • Delivery Log — Every webhook call is logged with status (sent, failed, blocked) and timestamp.
  • Rate Monitoring — The daily reset happens automatically. Exceeded limits block further dispatches until reset.

Conversion Tracking
