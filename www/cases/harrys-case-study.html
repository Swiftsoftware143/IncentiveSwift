📋 The Business Case

Harry's, an emerging direct-to-consumer brand entering the highly competitive men's grooming market, required a massive, organic pre-launch audience to scale demand instantly on launch day.

📋 The Challenge

Standard email collection landing pages suffer from low conversion floors. To build true launch velocity, they needed an automated model that would turn single visitors into passionate, organic distributors.

💡 The Strategy

They structured a gamified milestone referral page. Instead of relying on a random raffle sweepstakes, they utilized guaranteed, progressive rewards to completely change sharing behaviors.

  • Users arrived on a streamlined splash landing page and entered an email address to join the product waitlist.
  • Upon submission, the page immediately presented a unique personal tracking link paired with a clear, gamified milestones tracker.
  • Participants unlocked physical rewards progressively: 5 referrals unlocked free shaving cream, 10 referrals unlocked a razor setup, and 50 referrals unlocked a full one-year supply of blades.
