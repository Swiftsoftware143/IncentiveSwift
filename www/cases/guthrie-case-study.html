📋 The Business Case

Guthrie & Sons, a provider operating within the local home services sector, required a scalable framework to drive premium installation contracts without paying high listing commissions to standard local aggregator directories.

📋 The Challenge

Standard home service referral programs rely on single-sided future discounts, which create a subtle "referral gap"—existing customers often feel uncomfortable recommending high-cost services to friends for a selfish kickback.

💡 The Strategy

They deployed an optimized double-sided referral engine to remove social friction by completely balancing rewards for both parties.

  • Existing customers were provided an easy digital entry point to submit neighbor or peer recommendations.
  • Instead of small coupon structures, the campaign focused on heavy value alignment: upon any successful new system equipment installation or major repair, both parties immediately unlocked an identical $250 incentive credit.
  • By prioritizing absolute equity, the program changed recommending a service from a transaction into an act of mutual benefit.
