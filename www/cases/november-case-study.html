📋 The Business Case

November Bicycles, a manufacturer specializing in custom carbon bicycle wheelsets and components, wanted to create massive marketing momentum for a fresh product rollout without relying on ongoing paid ad spend.

📋 The Challenge

Highly specialized consumer industries often find it difficult to scale past their existing audience clusters. They needed a strategic engine that would compel passionate catalog advocates to aggressively market the contest to outside lookalike prospects.

💡 The Strategy

They introduced a structured viral action-stacking sweepstakes. By shifting from a simple registration setup to a multi-entry referral framework, they maximized performance over a 30-day window.

  • They chose a highly coveted, premium piece of catalog product inventory to serve as the singular main grand prize.
  • To gain additional raffle entry tickets, users were seamlessly incentivized to complete micro-sharing events, including broad social media distributions and direct email shares.
  • This action stacking turned a typical passive sweepstakes into an active viral engine where users multiplied their entry numbers by introducing new, high-intent prospective buyers.
